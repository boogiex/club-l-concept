01 / My approach

Five things, one
line of thought.

I don’t treat these as separate jobs. Each one makes the next possible, and the value sits where they meet.

  1. 1

    Campaigns

    A click is a strong signal of intent. Someone saw a dress in a paid social ad and tapped. I look at the creative, the audience and whether the page they land on continues what the ad promised.

    The question I ask: what did they come here for?
    Paid socialCreativeAudiencesLanding pages
  2. 2

    Marketing technology

    The signal only matters if the technology behind the scenes passes it on. Tracking links, ad platform connections, consent and first-party data tools are what tell the website which ad they clicked and why they arrived.

    The question I ask: does the site know why they arrived?
    Tracking linksAd platform connectionsConsent modeCustomer data platform
  3. 3

    Data

    Once context travels, behaviour becomes readable: product views, size selection, wishlist, add to bag, drop-off. That is where intent turns into hesitation.

    The question I ask: where does intent turn into hesitation?
    First-party dataFunnel analysisSegmentationGA4
  4. 4

    Attribution

    Platform, web analytics and order data tell different stories, and budget follows the one that gets believed. I want to know what a campaign caused, not just what it touched.

    The question I ask: are we crediting the right things?
    Last click vs multi-touchIncrementalityMER / ROASMarketing mix modelling
  5. 5

    Revenue

    It all comes back to revenue: how much each visit is worth, the margin on it, how much people spend per order and whether they come back. A drop gets judged on profit and lifetime value, not clicks.

    The question I ask: what changed, and would it have happened anyway?
    Revenue per visitAverage order valueRepeat purchaseLifetime value

Each of these is useful on its own. Connected, they show what a campaign really earned, so a weekly drop can be judged and improved while it is still live. That is the thinking I would bring to Club L.

03 / Testing the thinking — a real customer journey

High intent has arrived.
What happens next?

I approached Club L as a customer who had already shown intent: I arrived from a specific Instagram ad and wanted to find the product that had caught my attention.

The question I wanted to answer

Once someone has clicked through from paid social, how does the onsite journey nurture that interest? I followed the path, looked for the advertised item and noticed how much effort was required to reconnect with it. This is an opportunity, not a criticism: any retailer with a busy weekly drop has gaps like this, and they are the quickest place to find upside.
